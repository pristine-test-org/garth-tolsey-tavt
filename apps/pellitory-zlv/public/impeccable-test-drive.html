Why it helps

Pellitory keeps one calm list in front of you, so the first thing you see each morning is what needs doing today and nothing that can wait until tomorrow.

Three reasons people stay

  • One page, one list: nothing to configure before you start.
  • Finished items stay visible with a tick, so progress is easy to see.
  • The list is shared, so everyone is looking at the same thing.

The list is read live each time you open the page.
